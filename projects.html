Academic coursework

Polynomial Regression & Model Selection

An experiment comparing polynomial degrees under a noisy data-generating process. The work considered validation RMSE and cross-validation RMSE to discuss the trade-off between fitting and generalisation.

Reported result from the notebook: degree 4 had the lowest validation RMSE (0.127249), while degree 9 had the lowest cross-validation RMSE (0.112186). These are results from this particular experiment, not a universal rule.
