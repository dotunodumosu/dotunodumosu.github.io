Context

Aegis Wealth Terminal is a collaborative project intended to support financial advisors and wealth-management workflows. My contribution is focused on quantitative research and the reliability of portfolio-risk calculations—not the completion of the entire application.

Work reported to date

  • Developed a portfolio-level daily-returns processing pipeline.
  • Implemented Value at Risk (VaR) and Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR) calculations in the research work.
  • Updated methodology notes and progress documentation to make assumptions and implementation choices easier to review.
  • Worked through a Git-based feature branch and pull-request workflow.

Why this matters

Risk metrics are only useful when the data preparation and assumptions behind them are clear. The work therefore needs attention to return-series alignment, missing observations, interpretation of tail risk and the distinction between a calculation and an investment recommendation.

Limitations and next steps

This case study is a snapshot of work in progress. Before treating the metrics as production-ready, the methodology should be reviewed against the intended use, data conventions, edge cases and the team’s acceptance criteria. No investment performance or client outcome is claimed here.

Responsible interpretation: VaR estimates a loss threshold for a stated horizon and confidence level under a specified method. CVaR estimates the average loss in the tail beyond that threshold. Neither measure guarantees a maximum loss.
