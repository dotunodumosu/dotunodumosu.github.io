About

A practical, research-minded approach to data and finance.

I combine postgraduate study in computational modelling with professional experience in banking and credit operations.

My background

I am pursuing an MSc in Computational Modeling in Technology and Finance at Southern Federal University (SFedU), Russia. My academic interests sit at the intersection of mathematical modelling, quantitative finance, data analytics and machine learning.

Before my postgraduate studies, I worked in banking roles related to business banking and credit facilities. That experience shaped my interest in how data quality, risk assessment and analytical tools can improve financial decisions.

I aim to build systems and analyses that are not only technically sound, but also understandable, reproducible and useful to the people who rely on them.

Areas I want to contribute to

  • Quantitative and financial analytics
  • Credit risk modelling and explainable machine learning
  • Computational modelling and simulation
  • Data cleaning, validation and decision support
  • Research that connects mathematical methods with real-world financial questions
